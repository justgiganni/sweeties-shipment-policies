Shipment Text Terms

This program is not live. Effective when the approved program launches.

By choosing shipment texts from Sweeties Bake Shop LLC, you agree to automated tracking notices for the shipments you request. Text enrollment is optional and is not a condition of purchase. It does not enroll you in marketing messages.

Usually one tracking notice is sent per shipment. Confirmation and service replies may add messages. Frequency varies with your shipments. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to cancel texts. Reply HELP or email info@sweetiesbakeshop.com for help. After cancellation, you must give fresh written permission for this shipment-text program before rejoining. If your number is also blocked by the carrier, text START or UNSTOP to the shipment number to remove that block.

Messages depend on carrier availability and can be delayed. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. For mobile-data handling, see the Shipment Text Privacy Policy.
