Shipment Text Privacy Policy

This describes the proposed SMS program, not every website or business process.

We use your mobile number, shipment reference and messaging preferences to provide requested tracking updates, handle replies and maintain consent and cancellation records. Text delivery providers process mobile numbers and message content to operate the service.

We do not sell mobile information or share it for advertising or promotional use. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties.

We limit access to people and providers who need it for this service and retain records only as needed to operate it and meet applicable requirements. Reply STOP to cancel future texts. For privacy questions or a request about your information, email info@sweetiesbakeshop.com.
